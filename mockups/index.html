JSGP Eldercare Triage — single-screen mockups

Five visual directions for the same MVP: a ranked caseload a One Care caseworker opens at shift start. Each is a working mockup — click a resident to drill down, and press Simulate incident to fire a live fall and watch the list re-rank.

Shared design rule across all five: acute events (a fall) always preempt at the top and look unmistakably different; chronic anomaly scores rank the calm caseload beneath. Every rationale is templated from the actual sensor features — the wording may be LLM-smoothed, the "why" never is.
Direction 1

Clinical Calm

Soft neutrals, muted teal/slate, generous whitespace. Reads like a trusted care tool, not an alarm panel.

Best when: you want low-anxiety and it must age well on stage.
